Sheet 04 · how a sheet gets built

Layer by layer.

Layers0 / 5 on

A-SURVEYOFF

Measured walls, services and datum levels. Out-of-square rooms are flagged before a panel is cut.

J-CARCASSOFF

Bays, doors and drawers set out on the grid from the architect’s intent.

J-DIMSOFF

Overall and chain dimensions, tolerances and fixing positions, readable at the bench.

J-NOTESOFF

Material, finish and hardware callouts keyed to the schedule and the cutting list.

REV-COFF

Clouded revisions and RFIs tracked, so the shop only ever works from the latest sheet.
